--- Interpretation notes (fill in after inspecting the tables above) ---
- If classical also degrades at the same difficulty level as quantum,
  the task itself is becoming genuinely harder (not a quantum-specific
  weakness) — expected and uninteresting.
- If quantum degrades notably FASTER than classical at a given knob,
  that isolates a real quantum-encoding weakness on that specific axis
  (compression sensitivity to cluster tightness, or to query noise).
- If random projection degrades faster than PCA specifically as task
  difficulty increases, that would mean PCA's advantage isn't fixed —
  it may matter MORE exactly when the task gets harder, which is the
  regime that matters most for a real deployment.
