--- Interpretation ---
Both curves rise monotonically with n_qubits and neither plateaus
within the tested range (statevector simulation cost, O(2^n_qubits),
makes going much further intractable without real hardware).
This is itself informative: no early plateau means qubit count is
a genuine bottleneck here, not something already saturated at low n.

PCA reaches the classical Recall@1 ceiling (1.000) by n_qubits=12-14,
while random projection is still climbing at n_qubits=14 (0.889).
Random DOES continue improving toward the ceiling, not stuck — this
is a genuine qubit-EFFICIENCY gap (PCA needs fewer qubits for the
same retrieval quality), not evidence that random projection is
fundamentally incapable.

Caveat: single synthetic corpus design (Gaussian blob clusters).
Whether this generalizes to real embedding distributions (e.g. actual
sentence/document embeddings, which are not isotropic Gaussians) is
untested — this result characterizes the ENCODER, not real-world data.
